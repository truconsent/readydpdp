Statutory exposure

What non-compliance actually costs

The DPDPA's Section 33 schedule, in full — not the one figure everyone quotes.

ViolationProvisionMaximum penalty
Failure of reasonable security safeguards (data breach)Section 8(5)Up to ₹250 Cr
Failure to notify the Board / affected persons of a breachSection 8(6)Up to ₹200 Cr
Breach of additional obligations for children's dataSection 9Up to ₹200 Cr
Breach of Significant Data Fiduciary obligationsSection 10Up to ₹150 Cr
Any other breach of the Act or RulesResiduaryUp to ₹50 Cr

Under Section 33(3) the Board can double a penalty on aggravating factors. Substantive obligations phase in 14 Nov 2026 – 14 May 2027 — the schedule above is what applies once they do.
