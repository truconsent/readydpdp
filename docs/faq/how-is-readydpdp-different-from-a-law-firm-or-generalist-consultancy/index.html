How is ReadyDPDP different from a law firm or generalist consultancy?

A law firm can tell you whether a specific clause or interpretation is legally sound. A generalist consultancy can help with broad process design. ReadyDPDP sits specifically at the intersection of DPDPA legal obligations and operational implementation — we don't just advise on what the law requires, we design and help build the actual consent flows, data registers, and fulfilment processes that make compliance real in your systems, informed by direct familiarity with the technical architecture DPDPA compliance actually runs on.
