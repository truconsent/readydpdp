Data Principal Rights

Do we need a self-service rights portal, or can we handle requests manually?

A dedicated self-service portal is not a strict legal requirement — a well-designed intake form with a defined internal routing and SLA process can satisfy the underlying obligation at moderate request volumes. As volume grows, the operational cost of manual handling typically makes a portal worthwhile on efficiency grounds alone, but that is a business decision layered on top of, not required by, the base compliance obligation.
