Significant Data Fiduciary

Does SDF designation apply to the whole company or a specific product?

Designation can be made in respect of a Data Fiduciary, or a class of Data Fiduciaries — the precise scope of a given designation depends on how it is notified. Organisations with multiple, meaningfully distinct business units or products should not assume a designation automatically covers or excludes any specific product without checking the actual terms of the notification that applies to them.
