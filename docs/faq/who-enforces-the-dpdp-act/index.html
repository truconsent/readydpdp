General DPDPA

Who enforces the DPDP Act?

The Data Protection Board of India is the primary enforcement body — receiving breach notifications, investigating complaints, issuing directions, and imposing penalties through its adjudicatory function. The Board operates largely as a digital-first office. Sector regulators (RBI, IRDAI, TRAI, healthcare regulatory bodies) continue to enforce their own existing data-related rules independently and in parallel — DPDPA enforcement does not replace or override sectoral regulatory oversight, meaning organisations in regulated sectors answer to both the Board and their sector regulator.
