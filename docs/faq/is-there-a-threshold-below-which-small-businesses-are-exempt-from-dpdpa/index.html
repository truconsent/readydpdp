General DPDPA

Is there a threshold below which small businesses are exempt from DPDPA?

The Act allows the Central Government to notify exemptions for specific classes of Data Fiduciaries, including on grounds such as the volume and nature of personal data processed — which could, in principle, cover certain categories of startups or small businesses. However, no broad, automatic small-business exemption exists in the Act's core text itself, and any such exemption would need to be specifically notified. Organisations should not assume exemption based on size alone; the obligations apply based on the nature and volume of personal data actually processed, not just headcount or revenue.
