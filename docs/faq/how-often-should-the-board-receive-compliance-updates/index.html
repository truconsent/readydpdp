DPO & Governance

How often should the board receive compliance updates?

We recommend at minimum an annual formal briefing, with an additional update ahead of any major event that changes your data risk profile — a funding round, IPO process, new market entry, significant product launch, or M&A transaction. Significant Data Fiduciaries, given the board-reporting DPO structure the Act requires, should expect a more frequent cadence than that baseline.
