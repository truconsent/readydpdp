Penalties & Enforcement

Can an organisation appeal a Data Protection Board penalty?

Yes — a person aggrieved by a Board order or direction can appeal to the Appellate Tribunal, which the Act designates as the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal (TDSAT). This provides a defined judicial-style review layer above the Board's own adjudicatory decisions.
