Data Principal Rights

What rights does a Data Principal have under the DPDPA?

A Data Principal has the right to access a summary of their personal data and the processing activities undertaken, the right to correction and erasure of their data, the right to grievance redressal, the right of nomination (designating someone to act on their behalf in the event of death or incapacity), and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Alongside these rights, the Act also specifies certain duties for Data Principals, such as not making frivolous or false complaints.
