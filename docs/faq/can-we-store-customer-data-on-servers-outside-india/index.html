Cross-Border Transfer

Can we store customer data on servers outside India?

Generally yes — the DPDPA permits cross-border transfer of personal data by default, restricted only to countries or territories the Central Government has specifically notified as restricted. This is a more permissive default than many organisations expect, especially those familiar with GDPR's adequacy-first model. However, sector-specific localisation rules — most notably RBI's requirements for payment system data — apply independently and can require India-only storage regardless of the DPDPA's general permissiveness.
