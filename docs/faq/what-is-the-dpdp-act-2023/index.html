General DPDPA

What is the DPDP Act 2023?

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) is India's comprehensive data protection law, governing how organisations collect, process, store and share the digital personal data of individuals in India. It establishes the roles of Data Fiduciary, Data Processor and Data Principal, sets out consent and notice requirements, grants Data Principals specific rights over their own data, creates the Data Protection Board as an enforcement body, and sets financial penalties for non-compliance reaching up to ₹250 crore for the most serious violations. It replaces the more limited data protection provisions that previously existed under India's IT Rules, and represents the country's first dedicated, comprehensive privacy statute. Many of its operative details — breach notification timelines, Consent Manager registration criteria, and similar procedural matters — are being filled in through the draft DPDP Rules, 2025, which are expected to be finalised and phased in over time. Practically, this means the Act sets the principles and obligations, while the Rules set the specific mechanics organisations must follow to comply with them.
