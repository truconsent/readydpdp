Industry

Manufacturing

Lower consumer-facing exposure but significant employee, dealer and IoT sensor data footprint.

Where the risk concentrates

  • Employee personal data (biometric attendance, payroll, health records) is often under-governed
  • Dealer and distributor personal data is frequently exchanged without formal agreements
  • IoT and factory-floor sensor data increasingly intersects with personal data where operators are identifiable
  • Legacy ERP and HR systems predate any DPDPA-aligned retention discipline
  • Vendor and contractor access to personal data is rarely audited
