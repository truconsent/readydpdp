Industries

Sector-Specific DPDP Compliance

DPDPA compliance looks different by sector. Here is how we frame the risk, and the services that matter most, for the industries we work in most.

BFSI

High-volume, high-sensitivity personal and financial data processing under simultaneous DPDPA and RBI/IRDAI oversight.

Healthcare & Life Sciences

Health data processing that combines DPDPA obligations with medical confidentiality norms and, frequently, Legitimate Uses exemptions for emergency care.

EdTech

Children's data at the centre of the product, triggering the DPDPA's strictest consent category.

E-commerce / D2C

High-frequency consent collection across marketing, personalisation and logistics, at consumer scale.

SaaS / IT Services

B2B and B2B2C products where the company is frequently both a Data Fiduciary for its own users and a Data Processor for enterprise customers.

Manufacturing

Lower consumer-facing exposure but significant employee, dealer and IoT sensor data footprint.

Government / PSU

Unique exemption pathways under the Act alongside large-scale citizen data processing responsibilities.

Insurance

Health, financial and beneficiary data combined with actuarial profiling, under IRDAI and DPDPA oversight together.

Telecom

Location, call and usage data at national scale, combined with existing sector-specific regulatory obligations.

Media & Entertainment

Audience data, viewing behaviour and increasingly personalised content recommendation engines.

HR-Tech / Staffing

Candidate and employee personal data, including background checks and, increasingly, algorithmic screening tools.

Real Estate / PropTech

Buyer, tenant and financing data shared across a fragmented ecosystem of brokers, developers and lenders.

Logistics

Delivery, address and contact data at extremely high transaction volume, shared across multi-party fulfilment networks.
