Industry

Telecommunications

Location, call and usage data at national scale, combined with existing sector-specific regulatory obligations.

Where the risk concentrates

  • Call detail records and location data are highly sensitive and heavily used for secondary analytics
  • Existing telecom-specific regulations must be reconciled with DPDPA obligations rather than treated as a substitute
  • Partner and reseller networks create extended data-sharing chains
  • SIM registration and KYC processes are high-volume collection points needing consistent consent architecture
  • Law-enforcement and lawful-interception obligations intersect with, but do not eliminate, DPDPA duties for other processing
