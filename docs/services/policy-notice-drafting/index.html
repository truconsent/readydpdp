Service

Policy & Notice Drafting

Plain-language privacy notices, internal data protection policies and process documentation that satisfy DPDPA disclosure requirements without legal jargon.

What it is

Section 5 of the DPDPA requires that notice be given in clear and plain language, itemising the personal data collected and the purpose of processing, before or at the time of collection. Most existing privacy policies were written for GDPR, by a law firm, for a lawyer to read. We rewrite your external notices and internal policies so they are legally sound and genuinely understandable to the people they govern.

Who needs this

Organisations whose privacy policy has not been rewritten since before DPDPA, or that operate without documented internal data handling, retention, or access-control policies.

Our process

  1. Content audit

    We review existing notices and policies against DPDPA disclosure and content requirements.

  2. Purpose-level notice drafting

    We draft itemised, purpose-specific notice content rather than a single catch-all disclosure.

  3. Internal policy suite

    We draft or update the internal policy set — data retention, access control, acceptable use, vendor management — that supports your external commitments.

  4. Plain-language review

    Every document is reviewed for readability against a plain-language standard, not just legal accuracy.

  5. Multi-language localisation guidance

    We advise on which scheduled languages your notice should be available in given your user base.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a separate notice for each product?

If products collect meaningfully different data for different purposes, yes — a single blended notice tends to fail the specificity requirement. We assess your product portfolio and recommend the right notice architecture.

What language must the notice be in?

The Act requires notice content to be available in English or any language specified in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution, at the Data Principal's option. We help you decide which languages are practically necessary based on your user base.

Will you also handle the website implementation?

We deliver the content and UX specification; implementation is typically handled by your engineering or web team, with our review before launch.

Deliverables

  • Redrafted privacy notice(s), purpose-itemised
  • Internal data protection policy suite
  • Retention schedule template
  • Plain-language review checklist for future updates

Timeline
