Glossary

Deemed Consent

An earlier drafting-era term now generally absorbed into the DPDPA's "Legitimate Uses" provisions covering consent-free processing scenarios.

Earlier drafts of India's data protection legislation used the term "deemed consent" for scenarios where consent is presumed rather than explicitly obtained — medical emergencies, employment purposes, compliance with law, and similar situations. In the enacted DPDPA 2023, this concept is expressed through the specific, enumerated Legitimate Uses provisions rather than a standalone "deemed consent" clause. The substance is similar — certain processing does not require the standard consent flow — but the precise conditions attached to each Legitimate Use in the final Act are what actually govern, and those conditions should be checked directly rather than relied on from memory of earlier drafts.
