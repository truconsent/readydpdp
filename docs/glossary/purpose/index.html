Glossary

Purpose

The specific, disclosed reason personal data is collected or processed — the unit of granularity the DPDPA's notice and consent requirements are built around.

A purpose is the specific, itemised reason personal data is being collected — "process your order," "verify your identity for KYC," "send you promotional offers" — rather than a single catch-all justification like "provide our services." The DPDPA's notice and consent framework is fundamentally purpose-centric: notice must itemise the purpose, and consent must be specific to it. Maintaining a clean, non-overlapping purpose taxonomy across an organisation is foundational to nearly every other compliance artefact, from consent UI copy to a structured data register.
