Glossary

Automated Decision-Making

Decisions made about a Data Principal through automated processing, without meaningful human involvement — an area of growing regulatory attention.

Automated decision-making refers to decisions affecting a Data Principal made through automated or algorithmic processing with little or no human involvement — credit approval models, automated fraud flags, algorithmic content moderation. While the DPDPA does not yet impose the kind of explicit automated-decision-making rights found in GDPR, Significant Data Fiduciaries' obligation to evaluate the algorithmic systems they use for processing brings this squarely into scope for larger organisations, and it is an area we expect regulatory attention to sharpen as the Rules mature.
