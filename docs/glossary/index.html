DPDPA Glossary

68 plain-language definitions of DPDP Act 2023 and draft DPDP Rules terminology — from Data Fiduciary to Voluntary Undertaking.

Adjudicating Officer

The Data Protection Board member or officer empowered to conduct inquiries and impose penalties under the DPDPA.

Anonymization

The process of rendering personal data no longer identifiable to a specific individual, taking it outside the Act's scope — if done rigorously.

Appellate Tribunal

The forum for appeals against Data Protection Board orders — the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal (TDSAT) under the DPDPA framework.

Automated Decision-Making

Decisions made about a Data Principal through automated processing, without meaningful human involvement — an area of growing regulatory attention.

Automated Means

Any equipment capable of automatically operating in response to instructions given for performing specific tasks — the technical basis of the Act's "processing" scope.

Breach Notification Timeline

The window within which a Data Fiduciary must notify the Data Protection Board and affected Data Principals of a personal data breach.

Central Consent Engine

A rule-based service architecture that enforces the consent and purpose rules defined in a Digital Personal Data Register (DPDR) and Customer Data Journey Registry (CDJR) at runtime.

Child's Data

Personal data of an individual under 18, subject to the DPDPA's strictest consent and processing restrictions.

Collection Point

The specific touchpoint — a form, screen, script, or API — where personal data is originally captured from a Data Principal.

Consent Artefact

The structured, machine-readable record of a specific consent event — what was consented to, when, under which notice version, and how.

Consent Fatigue

The degraded quality of consent decisions that results from Data Principals being asked to consent too frequently, too vaguely, or too repetitively.

Consent Manager

A registered entity that lets Data Principals give, manage, review and withdraw consent across multiple Data Fiduciaries through one interface.

Consent Manager Registration

The process and criteria by which an entity becomes a registered Consent Manager with the Data Protection Board.

Consent Withdrawal Propagation

The technical and operational process of ensuring a consent withdrawal actually stops downstream processing across every system that received the data.

Cross-Border Data Transfer

Transfer of personal data outside India — permitted by default under the DPDPA, subject to any government-notified restrictions.

Data Audit

A periodic, independent review of a Data Fiduciary's data processing activities and compliance posture, required of Significant Data Fiduciaries.

Data Breach

Any unauthorised processing, or accidental disclosure, acquisition, sharing, or loss of access, of personal data that compromises its confidentiality, integrity or availability.

Data Element

A single, discrete field of personal data — such as an email address or PAN — and the base unit of granularity in a structured data register.

Data Fiduciary

Any person who alone or with others determines the purpose and means of processing personal data — the primary duty-bearer under the DPDPA.

Data Fiduciary Accuracy Obligation

The duty to ensure personal data used to make a decision affecting a Data Principal, or disclosed to another Fiduciary, is accurate and complete.

Data Fiduciary Contact Person

The individual or department a Data Principal can contact regarding their personal data, required of every Data Fiduciary.

Data Fiduciary Contact Publication

The requirement that Data Fiduciaries make contact details for data protection queries readily and prominently available to Data Principals.

Data Fiduciary Obligations

The umbrella set of duties the DPDPA imposes on every Data Fiduciary — notice, consent, security, accuracy, retention, and rights fulfilment.

Data Localisation

A requirement that certain data be stored or processed within a specific country's borders, distinct from and layered on top of DPDPA transfer rules.

Data Minimization

Collecting and retaining only the personal data genuinely necessary for the disclosed purpose — no more, "just in case."

Data Principal

The individual to whom the personal data relates — the person the DPDPA is designed to protect.

Data Principal Rights (DPR)

The umbrella term for the full set of rights the DPDPA grants individuals over their own personal data — access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, nomination and withdrawal.

Data Processor

An entity that processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary, under the Fiduciary's instructions.

Data Protection Board

The regulatory and adjudicatory body established under the DPDPA to enforce the Act, primarily operating digitally.

Data Protection by Design

The practice of embedding privacy considerations into product and system design from the earliest stage, rather than retrofitting compliance after launch.

Data Protection Impact Assessment

A structured evaluation of the necessity, proportionality and risk of a specific processing activity, mandatory for Significant Data Fiduciaries.

Data Protection Officer

The individual designated to represent a Data Fiduciary's data protection function — mandatory for Significant Data Fiduciaries.

Data Retention

The obligation to erase personal data once its purpose is fulfilled and retention is no longer necessary for a legal or business reason.

Data Sharing Disclosure

The obligation to disclose, as part of the right to access, the identities of Data Processors and other Data Fiduciaries a Data Principal's data has been shared with.

Deemed Consent

An earlier drafting-era term now generally absorbed into the DPDPA's "Legitimate Uses" provisions covering consent-free processing scenarios.

Digital Personal Data

The DPDPA's actual scope: personal data in digital form, and personal data collected offline that is later digitised.

Draft DPDP Rules, 2025

The subordinate legislation that operationalises the DPDPA 2023's framework provisions with specific procedural detail.

Employment Purposes

A recognised Legitimate Use allowing processing of employee personal data necessary for employment-related purposes without separate consent for each instance.

Exemptions

Specific categories of processing or entities the DPDPA exempts, wholly or partly, from particular provisions of the Act.

Grievance Redressal Officer

The individual responsible for responding to a Data Principal's grievance regarding the processing of their personal data.

Harm

A broadly defined concept under the DPDPA covering bodily, psychological, financial, reputational and other injury a Data Principal may suffer from data misuse.

Independent Data Auditor

A third-party auditor who conducts the periodic data audit required of Significant Data Fiduciaries, distinct from internal compliance review.

Instrumentality of State

A related concept to State Instrumentality, describing bodies substantially controlled by government that may fall within specific DPDPA exemption provisions.

Legitimate Uses

Specific scenarios under the DPDPA where personal data may be processed without obtaining consent, distinct from Deemed Consent.

Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)

The administering ministry responsible for the DPDPA's implementation, Rules-making, and the constitution of the Data Protection Board.

Notice

The disclosure a Data Fiduciary must give a Data Principal before or at the time of seeking consent, in clear and plain language.

Notice Language Requirements

The DPDPA requires notice content be available, at the Data Principal's option, in English or a language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution.

Penalties & Adjudication

The DPDPA's financial penalty regime, adjudicated by the Data Protection Board, with penalties reaching up to ₹250 crore for the most severe breaches.

Personal Data

Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to that data — the DPDPA's foundational scope term.

Processing

Any wholly or partly automated operation performed on digital personal data — collection, storage, use, sharing, disclosure, erasure, and more.

Profiling

Automated processing used to analyse or predict aspects of a Data Principal's behaviour, preferences or attributes.

Public Interest Processing

Processing carried out for functions like providing benefits, subsidies, licences, or services, which the Act treats through specific Legitimate Use and exemption provisions.

Purpose

The specific, disclosed reason personal data is collected or processed — the unit of granularity the DPDPA's notice and consent requirements are built around.

Purpose Limitation

The principle that personal data may only be processed for the specific purpose disclosed and consented to — not repurposed silently.

Purpose Taxonomy

A defined, non-overlapping list of processing purposes an organisation uses consistently across notices, consent flows, and data registers.

Reasonable Security Safeguards

The obligation to protect personal data through appropriate technical and organisational measures proportionate to risk.

Research, Archiving and Statistical Purposes Exemption

A specific exemption for processing carried out for research, archiving, or statistical purposes, subject to standards the Central Government may prescribe.

Right of Nomination

A Data Principal's right to nominate another individual to exercise their rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Right to Access

A Data Principal's right to obtain a summary of their personal data being processed and the processing activities undertaken.

Right to Correction and Erasure

A Data Principal's right to have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected, and to have data erased once no longer necessary.

Right to Grievance Redressal

A Data Principal's right to have a readily available means of registering a grievance about processing or rights fulfilment.

Rights Fulfilment SLA

The internally defined target turnaround time for responding to a specific type of Data Principal rights request.

Sensitive Personal Data

A nuanced concept: the DPDPA itself does not formally define a separate "sensitive" category, unlike GDPR or India's earlier SPDI Rules.

Significant Data Fiduciary

A Data Fiduciary designated by the Central Government for enhanced obligations, based on factors like data volume, sensitivity and risk.

State Instrumentality

A concept relevant to how certain exemptions and obligations apply differently to government bodies and their instrumentalities under the Act.

Verifiable Parental Consent

The heightened consent standard required before processing a child's personal data — consent must be genuinely verifiable, not merely self-declared.

Voluntary Undertaking

A commitment a Data Fiduciary can voluntarily offer to the Data Protection Board to address a compliance issue, potentially resolving proceedings without a full penalty order.

Withdrawal of Consent

A Data Principal's right to withdraw previously given consent at any time, with a withdrawal process as easy to use as the consent process.
