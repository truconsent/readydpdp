Glossary

Data Fiduciary Contact Publication

The requirement that Data Fiduciaries make contact details for data protection queries readily and prominently available to Data Principals.

Beyond simply designating a contact person, the Act expects that contact information be genuinely accessible to Data Principals — typically published prominently within a privacy notice or on a website — rather than technically existing but practically undiscoverable. Auditing whether required disclosures are actually easy for an ordinary user to find, not just technically present somewhere on a site, is a standard check in every Gap Assessment we run.
