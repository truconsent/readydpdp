Glossary

Harm

A broadly defined concept under the DPDPA covering bodily, psychological, financial, reputational and other injury a Data Principal may suffer from data misuse.

The Act defines harm broadly to include bodily harm, distortion or theft of identity, harassment, prevention of lawful employment, psychological manipulation, financial loss, restriction on free movement, discriminatory treatment, and other significant adverse effects. This broad definition underlies why obligations like reasonable security safeguards and breach notification are triggered by the risk of harm generally, not a narrow, fixed list of qualifying injuries.
