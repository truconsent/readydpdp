Glossary

Data Retention

The obligation to erase personal data once its purpose is fulfilled and retention is no longer necessary for a legal or business reason.

A Data Fiduciary must erase personal data once the purpose for which it was processed is no longer being served and retention is not necessary for legal compliance, unless the Data Principal withdraws consent or the purpose is no longer specified — subject to any longer retention period required by other applicable law. In practice, this requires a defined retention schedule per data category, not an indefinite default. It is one of the fields we build directly into a client's Digital Personal Data Register as Consent Expiry, so retention discipline is enforceable at the data-element level rather than left as an abstract policy statement.
